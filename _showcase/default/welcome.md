<h2>Welcome to Shunchang's Blog!</h2>
<p>
    I've written a few papers, but honestly they mostly show that I can do basic research and write decent summaries.
    I haven't yet done work that truly satisfies me.
    What I keep chasing is research that feels <strong>simple and beautiful</strong>, in its ideas, its structure, and the code, writing, and visuals that express it.
    I trust my sense of aesthetics, and it keeps growing with new experiences and feedback.
    That is what keeps me curious, and it is why I chose to do a PhD. I want to see how far I can go.
</p>
<p>
    Some people might call me an idealist. I don't love labels, but fair enough.
    I think of my life as a journey in three stages.
</p>
<ul>
    <li>
        <strong>Making a living.</strong> I'm not very materialistic, but I want the freedom to try different lifestyles, and maybe study art or music someday.
        I also want to make sure that neither my loved ones nor I have to give up what matters because of money.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Being seen.</strong> I hope people remember me, not for fame but for having done something meaningful.
        Recognition isn't the goal, only something that may come along the way.
    </li>
    <li>
        <strong>Making a difference.</strong> I want to help others, not by giving advice or telling my story, but by helping build a world where more people get the chance to shine,
        and where everyone can do what they love and become who they want to be.
    </li>
</ul>
