A little about me.

I've written a few papers, but honestly they mostly show that I can do basic research. I haven't yet done work that truly satisfies me. What I keep chasing is research that feels simple and beautiful, in its ideas, its structure, and the code, writing, and visuals that express it. That is what keeps me curious, and it is why I chose to do a PhD. I want to see how far I can go.

Some people might call me an idealist. I don't love labels, but fair enough. I think of my life as a journey in three stages.

  • Making a living. I'm not very materialistic, but I want the freedom to try different lifestyles, and maybe study art or music someday. I also want to make sure that neither my loved ones nor I have to give up what matters because of money.
  • Being seen. I hope people remember me, not for fame but for having done something meaningful. Recognition isn't the goal, only something that may come along the way.
  • Making a difference. I want to help others, not by giving advice or telling my story, but by helping build a world where more people get the chance to shine, and where everyone can do what they love and become who they want to be.
